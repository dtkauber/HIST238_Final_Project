To the Reader

~ ❧ ~

Welcome to the private journal of William Hutchinson, a fictional Philadelphia merchant living through the American Revolution. As a merchant, Hutchinson’s livelihood relies heavily on trade with his mother country Britain, but as tensions begin to rise in the American Colonies, Hutchinson watches the growing resentment toward British rule with concern. Hutchinson’s journal follows the events of the Revolution as they unfold in real-time through the eyes of a Loyalist, exploring how these radical changes impact his business, relationships, and overall livelihood.

Through Hutchinson’s perspective, this project will explore not only the division that the Revolution created amongst a colony and its ruler, but also the division amongst colonial neighbors. Hutchinson’s fictional lived experiences allow readers to see what liberty and freedom meant to the colonists who remained loyal to Britain despite everything going on around them.

Although Hutchinson and his stories are fictional, the entries in this journal are based on real Loyalist diaries, letters, and other historical resources. Alongside primary sources from the period, these fictional entries express the fear, difficulties, and actions of those who opposed the Revolution.
