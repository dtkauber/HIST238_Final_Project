### HIST238 Final Project

This is my final project for HIST238: The American Revolution, 1763 - 1815 at the University of North Carolina - Chapel Hill. The project surrounds the private journal of a fictional loyalist named William Hutchinson. Hutchinson is a Philadelphia merchant who fears the repercussions of his loyalty to Britain. This journal takes the reader into the events of the American Revolution through the eyes of Hutchinson, giving better perspective on the underrepresented Loyalist view during this time period.
